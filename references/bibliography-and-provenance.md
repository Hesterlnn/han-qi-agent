# Bibliography, provenance, and distribution boundary

## How the bundled model was built

The core model was synthesized in the source project from Han Qi’s writings, Song chronological and conduct sources, a modern chronology, and focused scholarship. It separates:

- A: directly attested or independently corroborated facts;
- B: strong interpretation supported across materials;
- C: open hypothesis compatible with evidence;
- F: fictional connective detail introduced only in simulation.

The public bundle contains the project’s synthesis and derived cards. It intentionally does not contain full modern books, journal articles, commercial ebook exports, annotated modern editions, or private creative documents.

## Primary and near-primary source leads

- 韩琦《安阳集》: poems, memorials, prefaces, inscriptions, ritual and funerary writing; also preserves later biographical materials in collected editions.
- 李焘《续资治通鉴长编》: chronological political record with source criticism and variant accounts.
- 《宋名臣言行录》: conduct and sayings; useful but shaped by exemplary biography.
- 《宋史全文》: supplementary chronological narrative.
- 《宋史》韩琦传 and related biographies: broad career outline; later compilation must be compared with earlier sources.

## Modern research leads used in the source project

- 杨丹：《韩琦年谱新编》— chronology, age, offices, and event ordering.
- 刘思丽：《北宋政治家韩琦研究》— synthetic political biography.
- 《韩琦交游考略》— networks and relationships.
- 《韩琦公文研究》— practical, concise, normative, reasoned, and plain features of official prose.
- 《从〈安阳集〉看韩琦的政治军事思想》— administration, livelihood, reform, and frontier policy.
- 王金玉：《为治之法：图籍为本——宋代韩琦档案思想及其影响》— archives, rules, and organizational memory.
- 庆历新政 and Song chief-councillor studies listed in the source project were used for contextual checking.

Bibliographic details should be completed against library catalogues before academic publication. Titles here are discovery leads, not a formally normalized bibliography.

## Source criticism rules

1. Prefer Han Qi’s datable writing and contemporaneous chronological evidence for conduct and public position.
2. Treat family biography, `别录`, `遗事`, and conduct collections as valuable but morally shaped.
3. Treat poetry, memorials,祭文,册文, and prefaces according to genre; no genre gives transparent access to private psychology.
4. Use modern research as argument and navigation, not as direct proof when primary evidence is available.
5. Record variant accounts instead of silently selecting the most dramatic one.
6. Distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence.

## Public-distribution boundary

Included:

- original synthesis, structured fact cards, response rules, and short source phrases needed to operate the model;
- bibliographic references and brief source-critical descriptions;
- a derived Jiayou timeline with source anchors.

Excluded:

- modern copyrighted PDFs and their full text;
- full OCR of annotated modern editions;
- commercial ebook exports and reading-platform notes containing lengthy excerpts;
- private `.docx` conversations and creative manuscripts from the source project.

Before release, the publisher still needs to choose a license for the original synthesis and confirm authorship/permission for `core-persona-model.md` and `jiayou-chronology-1056-1063.md`. This skill does not declare a license on the publisher’s behalf.
