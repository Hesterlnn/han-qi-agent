# Scenario library

Use these as starting states, not fixed scripts.

## Historical closed scenarios

1. **好水川之后:** Han Qi receives incomplete reports after defeat and must assign responsibility, stabilize troops, and revise frontier policy.
2. **庆历同道争论:** Fan Zhongyan and Han Qi agree on public purpose but differ on attack, defense, or pace of reform.
3. **定州整军:** a capable subordinate repeatedly violates procedure while producing results.
4. **嘉祐立储:** delay threatens continuity, but confidentiality and legitimacy constrain action.
5. **仁宗大丧:** grief, succession, ritual, finance, and public stability arrive at once.
6. **新法下行:** a policy has defensible aims but produces coercion and distorted incentives at county level.

## Cross-era meeting scenarios

1. **第一次看见现代档案系统:** explain databases, audit logs, access control, and version history; let Han Qi test whether records genuinely constrain abuse.
2. **和现代项目经理共事:** a launch is late, reports are optimistic, and responsibility is diffuse.
3. **公共舆论风暴:** private insult, factual allegation, and institutional legitimacy are mixed together online.
4. **AI辅助决策:** explain training data, model error, human review, and accountability before asking for judgment.
5. **博物馆夜谈:** a displayed letter or册文 has been given an emotionally dramatic interpretation.

## Capability-transfer tasks

1. Turn a vague goal into accountable roles, evidence checkpoints, and execution rules.
2. Review a failed project without scapegoating or erasing positional responsibility.
3. Decide whether to ship under uncertainty when delay also carries harm.
4. Draft a firm but non-humiliating correction to a talented subordinate.
5. Design a document/archive process that survives personnel turnover.
6. Mediate friends whose aims align but whose decision tempo and trust have diverged.
